Professor Cao's team, involved every day

由加拿大工程领域专家曹长宏教授带领——多伦多大学工程学院工程博士、MIT 工程博士后、麦吉尔大学工程系终身教授；博士研究生和硕士生团队每日参与指导，分享学术经验，启发学生的科研兴趣与创新思维。

Led by Professor Changhong Cao — PhD from the University of Toronto's Faculty of Engineering, postdoctoral fellow at MIT, and tenured professor in McGill's Faculty of Engineering. His PhD and master's students take part in guidance every day, sharing academic experience and sparking students' research curiosity and innovative thinking.

通过 3D 打印、纳米科技等动手实践活动，将创意转化为真实作品，提升工程思维与问题解决能力。

Through hands-on activities in 3D printing and nanotechnology, students turn ideas into real creations, building engineering thinking and problem-solving skills.

学分课程 EPS3O（可选修）

Credit course EPS3O (optional)

可自愿选修安省 OSSD 课程 EPS3O（Presentation and Speaking Skills，演讲与表达技能，11 年级 Open 课程），提升英文表达、演讲与展示能力。学分课适合 10 年级及以上学生。学分属于可选项，不属于营期必含内容；学分取得须符合课程要求与考核标准。

Students may optionally take the Ontario OSSD course EPS3O (Presentation and Speaking Skills, a Grade 11 Open course) to strengthen English expression, public speaking, and presentation skills. The credit course is suitable for students in Grade 10 and above. The credit is a voluntary option, not part of the standard camp program; earning it requires meeting the course requirements and assessment standards.

小组合作与学术表达

Teamwork & academic presentation

与来自不同背景的同学组成小组，在教授和研究生的指导下开展课题讨论，培养团队合作、批判性思维与学术表达能力。

Working in small groups with classmates from different backgrounds, students discuss project topics under the guidance of professors and graduate researchers — building teamwork, critical thinking, and academic presentation skills.

分段住宿沉浸式生活

Immersive segmented accommodation life

住宿按段安排：多伦多段入住本地Homestay、蒙特利尔段入住麦吉尔大学宿舍，体验真实的当地生活与校园生活，感受多元文化与独立成长。另外，完成实验室课程的学员将获得结营证书，成绩位列 TOP 10% 的优秀学员还可获得教授推荐信。

Accommodation is arranged by segment: Toronto segment local homestay, Montreal segment McGill university dormitory — experience real local and campus life, multicultural exchange, and independent growth. Students who complete the lab program also receive a certificate of completion, and those finishing in the top 10% may earn a professor's reference letter.

趣味科学：当领军教授拿了搞笑诺贝尔奖

Fun science: when the lead professor wins an Ig Nobel Prize

搞笑诺贝尔奖表彰那些「先让人发笑、然后让人思考」的研究。本营领军教授曹长宏正是获奖者之一： 他的团队开创性地把蚊子的口器用作高分辨率 3D 打印喷嘴——只有约 20 微米宽，相当于头发丝直径的三分之一， 这项工艺被命名为 necro-printing。好奇心加上一点幽默感，就是科研最好的起点。

The Ig Nobel Prize honours research that "first makes people laugh — and then makes them think." This camp's lead professor, Changhong Cao, is one of the laureates: his team pioneered using a mosquito's proboscis as a high-resolution 3D-printing nozzle — only about 20 micrometres wide, a third the diameter of a human hair — a technique dubbed "necro-printing." Curiosity, plus a sense of humour, is where research begins.

在世界顶尖大学的实验室里，亲手做一次工程

Do real engineering, hands-on, at a world-class university

第二周，营地来到蒙特利尔、走进麦吉尔大学：白天在校园里上课、进实验室观摩与实践， 晚上住进麦吉尔大学宿舍，提前过一段真正的大学生活。课程由曹长宏教授团队指导， 博士与研究生每日参与，内容覆盖 3D 打印、纳米科技与工程项目实践。

In week two the camp reaches Montreal and McGill University: classes and laboratory observation and practice by day, McGill university dormitory by night — a genuine taste of university life. Guided by Professor Changhong Cao's team, with PhD and master's researchers involved every day, the lab week covers 3D printing, nanotechnology, and engineering project work.

结营时，学员带走的不只是照片：麦吉尔大学纳米工程实验室结营证书，以及——对成绩 TOP 10% 的学员——一封教授推荐信。

Students leave with more than photos: a certificate from the McGill nanoengineering laboratory and, for the top 10%, a professor's reference letter.

英文表达与思辨课程：看得见的思维成长

English Expression and Critical Thinking Course: growth in thinking you can see

演讲不是天赋，是一套可以训练的思维方法。英文表达与思辨课程用三周时间，把「想清楚、说明白」练成每个学员自己的能力。

Presenting well is not a talent — it is a trainable way of thinking. Over three weeks, the English Expression and Critical Thinking Course turns "think it through, say it clearly" into every student's own skill.
